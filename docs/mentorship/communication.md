

# Communication Expectations

- **Where We Chat**: Use our official community discussions or Discord/Slack channels for general questions.
- **Asynchronous Respect**: Remember that maintainers and mentors span multiple time zones. Please allow up to 24–48 hours for PR review feedback.
- **Be Kind and Constructive**: All participants must abide by our [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/nensii21/devlink/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
