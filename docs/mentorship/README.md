# Contributor Mentorship Program 🌟

Welcome to the **devlink** Contributor Mentorship Program! This initiative is designed to help first-time and beginner open-source contributors onboard smoothly, learn our development workflows, and successfully land their first contributions with guidance from experienced mentors.

---

## 🧭 Program Roadmap & Sections

1. **[Beginner Roadmap](./roadmap.md)**: A step-by-step path from setting up your local environment to making your first pull request.
2. **[Weekly Checklists](./checklists.md)**: Structured tasks for your first 2 to 4 weeks in the community.
3. **[PR Review Guidelines](./pr-guidelines.md)**: What our maintainers look for and how to ensure quick PR approvals.
4. **[Communication Expectations](./communication.md)**: Where we chat, how to ask questions, and community etiquette.
5. **[Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ](./faq.md))**: Quick answers to common contributor roadblocks.

---

## 👥 Mentor Assignment Guide

- **How to Get Assigned**: When you open your first issue or pick up a starter task labeled `good first issue`, you can request a mentor by commenting `/mentor` or asking in our community chat.
- **Mentor Role**: Mentors are here to unblock you, explain code architecture, review draft PRs, and provide constructive feedback.
- **Contributor Role**: Be proactive, communicate clearly when you are stuck, and keep your PRs focused and small.
