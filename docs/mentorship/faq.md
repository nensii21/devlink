# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Can I work on multiple issues at once?**  
A: As a first-time contributor, we strongly recommend focusing on one issue at a time to avoid context switching and ensure a smooth onboarding.

**Q: What should I do if I get stuck?**  
A: Don't hesitate to tag your assigned mentor or leave a comment on your pull request explaining where you are stuck.

**Q: How long does a PR review take?**  
A: Initial reviews usually happen within 48 hours.
